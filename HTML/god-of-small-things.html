The God of Small Things

by Arundhati Roy

The God of Small Things is an award-winning novel by Arundhati Roy, set in Kerala, India. It follows twins Estha and Rahel as they revisit the memories of their childhood and the events that changed their family forever. Through their experiences, the novel explores family relationships, forbidden love, social inequality, caste discrimination, and the lasting effects of painful memories. With vivid imagery, lyrical language, and a distinctive non-linear narrative, Roy shows how seemingly small moments can have life-changing consequences.
